     6. Keyword promotion mechanism — runs at program startup

     Scans .ai_learning/choices_learning.json entries, promotes high-frequency
     co-occurring keywords
     to "strong" status in pos_dictionary (choices_pos_dictionary.json).

     How it works:
     - Group all entries by word + user_choice (e.g. all "lead" → "led"
     decisions)
     - Count how often each surrounding word appears for each spelling
     - Words appearing frequently with spelling A and rarely with spelling B →
     strong keyword for A
     - Promoted keywords written to pos_dictionary under the spelling's
     context_keywords
     - Frequency threshold: word must appear in at least 3 entries for a given
     spelling to promote
     - Discrimination threshold: frequency for spelling A must be at least 2x
     frequency for spelling B

     When to run:
     - At startup, before processing begins
     - Pure Python JSON scan + dict counting — no spaCy, no AI, under 1 second
     - Add promote_strong_keywords() to ChoicesLearningAnalyzer
     (choices_learning.py)
     - Call from AIChoiceProcessor.init() (ai_choices.py) after learning storage
     init
